## Data Collection

### For Broad Rotation Overview (all 11 sectors)
1. **Performance Rankings:** `"S&P 500 sector performance 1 week 1 month 3 month 6 month YTD 2025 2026"` + `"sector ETF performance comparison XLK XLV XLF XLE XLY XLP XLI XLB XLU XLRE XLC"`
2. **Money Flows:** `"sector ETF fund flows inflows outflows [current month] [current year]"`
3. **Economic Cycle:** `"economic cycle phase 2025 2026 expansion contraction recession indicators"` + `"ISM PMI GDP growth leading economic indicators latest"` + `"Federal Reserve interest rate outlook policy"`

### For Specific Sector Deep Dive
1. **Sector Outlook:** `"<SECTOR> sector outlook 2025 2026 trends growth drivers"`
2. **Top Holdings:** `"<SECTOR ETF> top holdings weightings performance"`
3. **Valuation:** `"<SECTOR> sector valuation PE ratio historical average"`
4. **Earnings:** `"<SECTOR> sector earnings growth revenue estimates"`
5. **Relative Strength:** `"<SECTOR ETF> vs SPY relative strength performance ratio"`
6. **Top Stocks:** `"<SECTOR> sector top performing stocks momentum leaders"`
7. **Breadth:** `"<SECTOR> sector breadth advance decline new highs lows"` + `"<SECTOR> stocks above 50 day moving average 200 day"`
